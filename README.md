# The Fellowship of the Portfolio

*Being a true and faithful account of one hobbit's attempt to beat the market.*

---

Three Baskets for the Elven-kings under the sky,
Seven for the Dwarf-lords in their halls of stone,
Nine for Mortal Men doomed to buy the high,
One for the Dark Lord on his margin loan.

In the Land of Backtest where the Drawdowns lie.

---

## Of the Founding of the Repository

In a directory in the ground there lived a trading agent. Not a nasty,
dirty, wet directory, filled with the ends of deprecated worms and an oozy
smell of unpinned dependencies; nor yet a dry, bare, sandy directory with
nothing in it to sit down on or to `git blame`. It was a Python directory,
and that means tests.

It had a perfectly round `__init__.py`, like a porthole, painted green,
with a shiny yellow linter-badge in the exact middle.

## Of the Council

"You cannot pass!" cried the execution gate, and it smote the proposal
upon the bridge. "I am a servant of the Deterministic Flame, wielder of
the Decimal of Precision. The dark float shall not avail you, flame of
IEEE-754! Go back to the Shadow. **YOU SHALL NOT PASS.**"

The proposal fell, and did not submit.

## Of the Sorting

The hat was old, frayed, and had opinions about position sizing.

"Hmm," said the hat. "Difficult. Very difficult. Plenty of courage, I
see. Not a bad mind, either. There's talent, oh yes. And a thirst to
prove yourself against the S&P — but where to put you?"

"Not Slytherin," thought the portfolio. "Not leveraged ETFs."

"Not leveraged?" said the small voice. "Are you sure? You could be great,
you know, it's all here in your Sharpe ratio, and Slytherin will help you
on the way to greatness, no doubt about it —"

"**NOT SLYTHERIN!**"

"Well, if you're sure — better be **CASH RESERVE!**"

## Of the Prophecy

*The one with the power to vanquish the Drawdown approaches.*
*Born to those who have thrice defied the Backtest.*
*And the Drawdown will mark it as its equal,*
*but it will have power the Drawdown knows not —*
*namely, a 10% minimum cash reserve and an actual stop-loss.*

## Installation

One does not simply `pip install` into Mordor. Its requirements are
unpinned, its transitive dependencies are ever watchful. There is
deprecation there that does not sleep, and the Great CI is ever red.

```bash
python -m venv .venv          # the Grey Havens
pip install -r requirements.txt   # the long defeat
python -m pytest -q           # speak "friend" and enter
```
