# The Council of Elrond

*Adopted by the Free Peoples of the Repository, in the Third Age.*

"Strangers from distant lands, friends of old. You have been summoned
here to answer the threat of Unvalidated Alpha. Middle-earth stands upon
the brink of destruction; none can escape it. You will unite, or you will
fall. Each race is bound to this fate, this one doom."

"Well, what are we waiting for?" said the Junior Developer, and swung an
axe at the Roadmap. The axe shattered.

## Phase 1 — The Shire (COMPLETE)

Nothing happened here for sixty years and everyone was very happy. Merged.

## Phase 2 — Bree (COMPLETE AFTER INDEPENDENT CORRECTION)

Strider found four hobbits in a tavern committing directly to `main`.
He explained, at length, why this was unwise. Independent review
confirmed his finding and added a regression test.

## Phase 3 — Moria (COMPLETE, INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED)

They dug too greedily and too deep. What they awoke in the darkness was
a circular import. It has been extracted into seven kernel modules and
pinned by a symtable test so it can never wake again.

## Phase 4 — Lothlorien (COMPLETE)

"I know what it is you saw, for it is also in my mind. It is what will
come to pass if you should fail. The Fellowship is breaking. It has
already begun. He will try to take the Ring. You know of whom I speak.
One by one it will destroy them all."

"...it's technical debt, isn't it," said Frodo.

"It is technical debt," said Galadriel.

## Phase 5 — The Paths of the Dead (ACTIVE, OWNER-HEAVY)

The way is shut. It was made by those who are Dead, and the Dead keep
it. The way is shut. Also it requires an elevated PowerShell session,
and `Disable-ScheduledTask` returns "Access is denied" without one.

## Phase 6 — The Scouring (IN PROGRESS)

Product completeness. Fewer orcs than expected; considerably more
documentation.

## Phase 7 — The Grey Havens (DEFERRED, PENDING DATA PURCHASE)
