illegible. The last legible entries follow.*

## Standing decisions of the Dwarves of Khazad-dum

- We have retaken the mines. Balin is Lord of Moria.
- The Sessionne Handoffe keepeth losing our facts. We have moved them
  here. Do not move them back.
- `require_earnings_data` stayeth false. The feed knoweth not of ETFs,
  and would blockade the SOXX, and we have spent much research upon it.

## Machine-local knowledge, dearly bought

- The launch script must be used. A long-lived shell handeth thee a
  revoked key. We learned this the hard way, after the rotation.
- `Stop-ScheduledTask` succeedeth unelevated. `Disable-ScheduledTask`
  sayeth **Access is denied**. A merely stopped long-runner riseth again
  upon its own trigger, like something in the deep.
- **The backups lie upon the same disk as the database.** We know. We
  know. It is the smallest high-value slice and we have not done it.
- Drums. Drums in the deep.
